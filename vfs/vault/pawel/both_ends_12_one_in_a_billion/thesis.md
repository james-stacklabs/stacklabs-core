# Essay 12: One in a Billion, Every Day

> **Author**: Pawel Rudnicki | **Date**: 2026-10-20

## Core Thesis & Abstract
Rare events at scale: when something is tried 10 billion times a day, one-in-a-billion failures happen 10 times daily. Asymmetric disaster arithmetic.
