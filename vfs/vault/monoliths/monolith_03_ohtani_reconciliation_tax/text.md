# The Ramp-Up Tax vs. The Reconciliation Tax: The Ohtani Deferred Contract

## Abstract & Thesis
In capital allocation and systems architecture, upfront capital investment is predictable, but ongoing coordination and reconciliation taxes destroy long-term compound yield. The deferred structure mirrors zero-latency state synchronization.
