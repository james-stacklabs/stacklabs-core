# Don R. Swanson (1988): Migraine and Magnesium

> **Citation**: Perspectives in Biology and Medicine, 31(4): 526-557

### Silo A: Electrolyte Physiology & Cellular Biochemistry
Intracellular magnesium deficiency promotes cortical spreading depression, cerebral arterial spasms, and platelet serotonin release.

### Bridge B: Cerebrovascular Tone & Spreading Depression Thresholds
Magnesium acts as a natural physiological calcium channel blocker maintaining vascular tone and preventing neural excitotoxicity.

### Silo C: Clinical Neurology & Headache Medicine
Migraine sufferers exhibit subclinical systemic magnesium deficits during attacks, but routine serum testing fails to reveal intracellular depletion.

## The Swanson Bisociation Insight
Intracellular magnesium supplementation addresses the latent cellular excitability underlying migraine without requiring synthetic triptans.
