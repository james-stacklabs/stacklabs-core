# Don R. Swanson (1986): Fish Oil, Blood Viscosity, and Raynaud's Disease

> **Citation**: Library Quarterly, 56(2): 103-118

### Silo A: Marine Biochemistry & Dietary Lipids
Eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) and dietary fish oil significantly lower platelet aggregation and whole-blood viscosity.

### Bridge B: Blood Viscosity & Microcirculatory Fluid Dynamics
Elevated blood viscosity in cold-induced digital vasospasm causes ischemic tissue damage and capillary occlusion.

### Silo C: Vascular Surgery & Rheumatology
Raynaud's syndrome patients exhibit abnormally high blood viscosity and platelet reactivity under cold exposure, leading to digital necrosis.

## The Swanson Bisociation Insight
Dietary fish oil reverses the blood hyperviscosity driving Raynaud's ischemic attacks without requiring pharmaceutical vasodilators.
