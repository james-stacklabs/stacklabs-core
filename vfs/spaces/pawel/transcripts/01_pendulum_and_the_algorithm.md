# The Pendulum and the Algorithm: Why AI Makes Human Labor Unnecessary
**Author:** Pawel Rudnicki | **Publication:** Both Ends of the Curve (`bothendsofthecurve.com`)

## §1. The 90-Year Macroeconomic Pendulum
Across the FDR New Deal (1930s), the LBJ Great Society (1960s), and the Reagan supply-side turn (1980s), capital and labor traded bargaining power along a recurring pendulum. Even when capital held the upper hand, it still required human labor to manufacture, operate, and consume.

## §2. The Algorithmic Decoupling & Ghost GDP
Autonomous software breaks the historical loop: capital can now compound cognitive and operational output without recycling wages through households. This produces Ghost GDP—synthetic productivity that registers on corporate ledgers while evaporating from human wage circulation.

## §3. Thermodynamic Grid Ceilings
Software scaling assumptions eventually hit physical walls: 1,100 TWh power grid capacity, transformer lead times, and thermal dissipation ceilings.

## §4. Signal Saturation & Equilibrium Blindness
When synthetic generation floods every information channel at zero marginal cost, background noise rises to match the temperature of genuine signal—blinding ungrounded observers who lack independent physical anchors.
