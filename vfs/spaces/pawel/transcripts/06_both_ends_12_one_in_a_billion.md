# Essay 12: One in a Billion, Every Day
**Author:** Pawel Rudnicki | **Publication:** Both Ends of the Curve (`bothendsofthecurve.com`)

## §1. Poisson Tail Arithmetic at High Frequency
A one-in-a-billion failure sounds impossible to human intuition. But when an automated system executes ten billion operations per day, that one-in-a-billion edge case triggers ten times every twenty-four hours.

## §2. Cascading Micro-Dependencies
At scale, rare tail collisions are not anomalies; they are deterministic daily events requiring hard isolation gates.
