# Essay 9: Both Ends of the Curve
**Author:** Pawel Rudnicki | **Publication:** Both Ends of the Curve (`bothendsofthecurve.com`)

## §1. Nuclear Binding Energy vs. Gaussian Bell Curves
In physics, energy is released by fusion at the light end of the curve and fission at the heavy end—never from Iron-56 sitting comfortably in the middle.

## §2. Equilibrium Blindness in the Middle
Institutions that optimize for the warm, consensus middle of a distribution become thermally indistinguishable from their benchmark—losing all contrast until tail events strike from both extremes.
