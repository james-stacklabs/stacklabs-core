# The 20-Year Trading Model Shock & Downside Defense
**Author:** Pawel Rudnicki | **Publication:** Both Ends of the Curve (`bothendsofthecurve.com`)

## §1. Three Lines of Unexplainable Logic
An AI assistant refactored a 20-year production trading model and replaced a core risk calculation with three lines of opaque logic. In historical backtests, the replacement performed identically—or slightly better.

## §2. Capable vs. Feeling Capable
The allocator's existential trap is mistaking backtest fluency for structural comprehension. If you cannot explain why three lines of code behave as they do when liquidity vanishes, you do not own a model—you own an unhedged liability.

## §3. Regime Shift & Scale Breakdown
Single-equation and fractal backtest models assume scale invariance across market regimes. The moment macro boundary conditions shift, micro-correlations invert and black-box logic fails without tripping a single pre-trade alarm.
