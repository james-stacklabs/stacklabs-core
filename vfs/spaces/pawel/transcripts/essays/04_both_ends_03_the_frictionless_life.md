# Essay 3: The Frictionless Life
**Author:** Pawel Rudnicki | **Publication:** Both Ends of the Curve (`bothendsofthecurve.com`)

## §1. Which Difficulties Do the Actual Work
When software removes every tedious step from research and execution, it also removes the productive cognitive friction that forces an operator to notice flawed assumptions before capital is committed.

## §2. Parimutuel Crowd Distortion & Probability Blindness
At the racetrack window—just as in crowded derivatives books—human intuition mistakes random Poisson streaks and late tote-board momentum for structural edge. True discipline is the willingness to pass when surface conditions invalidate the speed figure.
