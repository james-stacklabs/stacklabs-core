# Essay 20: Owning a Piece (Local Silicon vs. Cloud Tolls)
**Author:** Pawel Rudnicki | **Publication:** Both Ends of the Curve (`bothendsofthecurve.com`)

## §1. The 1930s Rural Electrification Parallel
Compact models and deterministic POSIX filesystems on dedicated local hardware do for cognitive independence what rural electrification did in the 1930s.

## §2. Immunity to Rent-Seeking Toll Gates
Owning the physical storage plane and verification loop eliminates cloud API rate-limit lockouts and preserves complete provenance over proprietary research.
